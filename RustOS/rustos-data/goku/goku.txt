  The upgrade to the 1st sayian transformation, known to increse the 50 times increse in strength by another 2 times while also making the already standing up yellow hair sharper and adds electricity on top of it
-  SSJ 3:
  The form to "Go even further beyond". It multiplies the strength of the user by another 4 times compared to the Super Sayian 2 level, while making your hair grow even longer and sharper while multiplying the ammount of electricity around the user with a cost of your eyebrows while using this state.
