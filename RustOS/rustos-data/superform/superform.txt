A state which had its origin first on the classic adventures starting in Emerald hill. A basic parody of SSJ form of Son Goku has now been called THE form by most in the franchise. Everyone recognises Goku's SSJ form for its first apperance, yet this one still sits out up there in it being the icon of the verse - golden fur with upwards pointing spines accompanied by the fire-like glow of the aura radiating around him.. It's just iconic in basic terms and gives a bigger boost than the normal SSJ form by the use of the 7 Chaos Emeralds. More about this transformation at %Chaos Emeralds%
